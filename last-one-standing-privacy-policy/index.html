Privacy Policy — Last One Standing

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Last One Standing ("the App"), operated by Digital Twins LLC ("we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, and protects your information. It applies to all users of Last One Standing on the Google Play Store and the Apple App Store.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

1. Contact Information

  • Developer/Company: Digital Twins LLC
  • App Name: Last One Standing
  • Contact Email: service@digitaltwinsapps.com

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at the email above.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Player Identity

Last One Standing is a real-time multiplayer game. To place you in matches and keep your progress, the App creates a player identity the first time you open it:

  • A randomly generated identifier stored on your device, used to sign you in automatically
  • A display name that you choose, and the avatar you assemble in the App

We do not collect email addresses, passwords, phone numbers, or real names, and no account creation or sign-in is required. Because your identity is tied to the app installation on your device, uninstalling the App or clearing its data may make your progress unrecoverable.

Visible to other players: because this is a multiplayer game, your display name and avatar are shown to the other players in your match, on leaderboards, and to people who add you as a friend. Please do not use a display name that reveals personal information.

2.2 Gameplay Data

Collected during normal use of the App, on our game servers:

  • Match results, scores, response times, and how long you survived
  • Career totals and per-game statistics, win counts, and streaks
  • Rank, season standing, and leaderboard placement
  • Daily challenge entries and daily streak counters
  • Head-to-head records against players you have faced
  • Badges and achievements you have unlocked
  • Revive tokens, and any in-app ratings or votes you submit about games
  • Match replay data for matches you take part in

This data is used to run the game itself — matchmaking, elimination, leaderboards, progression, and replays.

2.3 Device and Diagnostic Information

  • Device model, operating system, and app version
  • IP address and general region (not a precise address)
  • Network timing measurements, used to keep real-time matches fair across different connections
  • Crash and error reports, used to diagnose problems
  • Advertising identifier — see Section 5

2.4 Push Notification Token

Push notifications are off by default. If you turn them on in Settings, we store a notification token issued by your device's operating system so we can send you reminders about daily challenges and your streak. Turning notifications off in Settings stops this.

2.5 Virtual Currency

Star Points are a virtual currency earned by playing. We store your balance and how it was earned or spent. We do not offer real-money prizes, and we do not collect payment information.

3. How We Use Your Information

  1. To provide and operate the App — run matches, matchmaking, leaderboards, friends, progression, and replays
  2. To improve the App — analyze how games are played so we can balance difficulty and fix problems
  3. To show ads — via Google AdMob (banner and rewarded ads only)
  4. To send notifications you asked for — only if you enabled them
  5. To ensure fairness, safety, and legal compliance — detect cheating and abuse, and comply with applicable law

4. Legal Bases for Processing (EEA/UK Users)

  • Performance of a contract — core game services (matches, leaderboards, progression)
  • Legitimate interests — improving the game, preventing cheating and abuse
  • Consent — personalized ads, and push notifications

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Google AdMob (Advertising)

  • Displays banner ads and optional rewarded ads
  • May collect device identifiers (including the advertising ID) and IP address for ad delivery and measurement
  • Where required by law, personalized ads are shown only with your consent; otherwise ads are non-personalized

Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5.2 Firebase Cloud Messaging (Notifications)

Used only to deliver the push notifications you opt in to. If you never enable notifications, no notification token is registered.

Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5.3 Game Servers

The gameplay data described in Section 2.2 is stored on game servers operated for the App. Traffic between the App and those servers is encrypted in transit.

6. Ads

  • Banner ads — a small banner at the bottom of the screen
  • Rewarded ads — entirely optional. You choose to watch one in exchange for a stated reward, such as a revive token. Declining costs you nothing.

The App does not show forced full-screen (interstitial) ads. You can reset or limit ad personalization at any time through your device's advertising settings.

7. Data Retention

  • Progress, career stats, and friends: retained while you use the App
  • Leaderboards and season standings: retained for the season, then archived
  • Match replay data: retained for a limited period tied to the match, then removed
  • Crash and error logs: retention varies by provider, typically 60–90 days

You can delete your data at any time (see Section 10).

8. How We Protect Your Data

  • Data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit
  • Game state is server-authoritative, which limits tampering with other players' results
  • Session tokens are stored on your device
  • Access to production systems is restricted to authorized personnel only

9. Children's Privacy

Last One Standing is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Where a player is identified as under 13, ad requests are configured for a child-safe experience — non-personalized ads, with ad content restricted to general audiences. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will delete it promptly.

10. Your Rights, and How to Delete Your Data

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, export, or restrict the processing of your data, and to withdraw consent for personalized ads or notifications.

You can delete your account and data yourself, from inside the App:

  1. Open Settings
  2. Tap Delete Account
  3. Confirm

This removes your player profile, statistics, progression, rank, badges, friend links, notification token, and virtual currency balance. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

If you prefer, you can instead email service@digitaltwinsapps.com with the subject line "Last One Standing data deletion request", including your display name and platform (Android/iOS). We will process the request within 30 days.

11. International Data Transfers

Our servers and third-party service providers may be located in countries other than your own. Where required by law, we put appropriate safeguards in place for those transfers.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. For material changes, we will notify users through the App.
